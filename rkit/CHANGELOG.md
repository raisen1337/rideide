# rkit changelog

rkit shows the entries newer than the rkit you have before you update it. Each `## <version>`
heading starts an entry; write for the people using rkit.

## 0.2.2

- ride, rterm and rdrop start from rkit with your own environment, fresh from Windows, rather than whatever started rkit; opened from another tool, they used to inherit its settings (and Claude Code in their terminals showed no colours).

## 0.2.1

- Chinese, Japanese and Korean names show instead of empty boxes.

## 0.2.0

- rdrop has a card of its own, and every server in the list opens in rdrop with one click.

## 0.1.0

- **rkit, one place for the suite.** Open ride or rterm, open a folder in ride, start any of rterm's shells, and reopen the folders you used lately in ride, on this computer or on a server.
- Every server in your SSH config, one click from ride or from an SSH session in rterm.
- Updates for each app on its own, with its changelog: see what's new, then update just that app.
